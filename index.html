How to spot a Flock camera

  • A small black box about the size of a hardcover book, mounted on its own pole or a light post, 8–12 ft up.
  • A solar panel on top or beside it, angled south.
  • Usually at intersections, neighborhood entrances, shopping-center exits and on-ramps, aimed at the lanes of traffic.
  • No visible lens dome or housing like a traffic camera; it looks like a plain box.

Tags to use (copy these into the editor):

man_made=surveillance
surveillance=public
surveillance:type=ALPR
surveillance:zone=traffic
camera:type=fixed
camera:mount=pole
manufacturer=Flock Safety
direction=  (bearing 0–359 the lens points, or e.g. NE)
operator=  (police dept or HOA, if known)

Stand where the camera is, note the compass bearing it faces, then open the editor at your current view, place a point, and paste the tags.
